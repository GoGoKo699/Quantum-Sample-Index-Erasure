# Scientific reading route

The task is to remove address labels from unknown coherent classical examples while retaining the superposition of values. The complete account is organized by the scientific steps.

1. [Model and main result](model.md): input interfaces, output conventions, theorem and range promise.
2. [Matching construction](construction.md): known index projection, all failed trials, unconditional output and streaming memory.
3. [Input harmonics](input_harmonics.md): why copies retain high weight in equal-tail representation sectors.
4. [Output harmonics](output_harmonics.md): why an ideal erased output, with arbitrary garbage, has little weight there.
5. [Copy lower bound](copy_lower_bound.md): Stinespring comparison, mean fidelity and finite constants.
6. [Resources](resources.md): source acquisition, gates, width, precision, and the inherited oracle comparison.
7. [Interpretation](interpretation.md): what the separation means and where it stops.

[Related work](../references/RELATED_WORK.md) credits the existing oracle theorem and representation machinery and distinguishes generic state-projection or Choi-program lower bounds from the structured injection promise. [Verification](../verification/README.md) records finite diagnostics and their limits.
